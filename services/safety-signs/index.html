Clear signs for hazards, emergency routes and workplace instructions. The final specification depends on your location, surface and viewing distance.

Materials and options

Suitable printed panels, reflective or photoluminescent materials where specified. Confirm the finish and indoor or outdoor exposure during the site assessment.

What affects the price?

Site requirements, applicable standards, visibility and quantities. Share photographs and approximate dimensions for an initial discussion; a firm price requires an agreed specification.

Installation and maintenance

Ask the team to confirm access requirements, electrical work where needed, cleaning instructions and the warranty for your selected materials in the written quotation.

What should I send for a quotation?

Your location, a frontage or installation photo, approximate width and height, quantity, preferred lighting and target date.
