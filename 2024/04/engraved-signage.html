Engraved Signs in Hyderabad – Durable, Professional & Long-Lasting Signage

Engraved sign boards are one of the most durable and premium signage options available. Using laser engraving or rotary engraving, text and logos are deeply etched into materials like plastic, acrylic, brass and aluminum — ensuring clarity that never peels or fades.

Since the design is physically cut into the surface, engraved plates are ideal for permanent labeling, name boards and industrial signage where long-term readability is essential.

How Engraved Signs Are Made

Most engraving sheets have a dual-layer surface. When the top layer is engraved, the contrasting bottom layer becomes visible — giving a sharp two-tone finish without printing.

  • Solid matte / glossy colour plates
  • Gold and silver metallic finish
  • Wood-grain texture
  • Marble pattern finish

FAQs

Do engraved nameplates fade? — No. The design is etched, not printed.

Can logos be engraved? — Yes, laser engraving supports detailed symbols.

Are engraved signs weatherproof? — Yes, especially brass, aluminum and engraving plastics.

Popular sizes for office doors? — 8×3, 10×4 and 12×4 inches (custom sizes available).

Is engraving better than vinyl? — For long-term use and premium finish, engraving is superior.
